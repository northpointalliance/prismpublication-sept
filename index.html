Advertisers and agencies

Who should buy Prism chat ads?

Brands and agencies that already run AI or conversation campaigns. Prism is extra inventory for that same product intent, placed on independent chatbots the large platforms do not cover. You do not start from a blank advertiser account. You port a live line: category, creative, destination, and a daily or lifetime cap.

We bill served, labeled cards only. If the user asks about the weather, the bot still answers and your ad stays out. If they ask for protein powder on a fitness bot, they can see a disclosed card under the reply. Email info@prismpublication.com with the campaign you already run. Creative rules live in ad submission.

Which categories can you buy on this page today?

Fitness, sleep tools, and productivity. Those are the three live slices in the sandbox. A human still reviews copy before anything ships on a real bot. Weapons, political campaigning, and medical advice sold as a bot recommendation stay out.

Fitness

Protein, bands, running gear, and training kit when the person is already talking about a workout. High commercial intent. Short questions. A natural place for a product card.

Sleep tools

Sound machines and masks when they want a practical sleep aid tonight. Adjacent wellness products only. Not a clinical claim and not a diagnosis.

Productivity

Notes, docs, and workspace tools on developer and knowledge-work bots. The Notion sample is how a software offer should sit next to an assistant reply.

What does a user actually see?

A labeled card under the assistant reply when the question is already about that product. Nothing extra when it is not. For engineers wiring a bot

How do you attach a bot without putting a key in the browser?

Keep the key on your server. After the assistant reply is ready, ask Prism for a card. The chat widget only draws HTML you already trust. There is no third-party ad tag in the transcript. If matching is too slow or the question is not close enough to an approved creative, send the reply alone. Import the client from sdk/prismClient.js in Node or a Worker. Email info@prismpublication.com for a publisher key.

Call displayAd with botId, format: "card", and context.topic set to the user question. Return title, description, URL, and the Sponsored via Prism label. Those field names are the integration contract. They are not the pitch to a media buyer.

What should a developer quote, and how does a buyer hear it?

Serve only when the question is close enough to the ad copy, finish matching in under 120 milliseconds, and label every card. The three rows below are the public floors. Cosine similarity 0.65 and the latency budget are how the matcher is built. "Close enough" and "the chat does not stall" are how a media lead should hear the same rules. Null means no card and no bill.
