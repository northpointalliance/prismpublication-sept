Advertisers and agencies

Who should use the sandbox?

Advertisers who want to test a contextual campaign against a real question, before that card sits next to a live bot reply. You are not starting from a blank brief. Bring the campaign you already run: category, creative, destination, and a daily or lifetime cap. Prism puts that same story on independent chatbots. Google Ad Manager is available. Keep GAM on the sites and apps you already use. On chat, the publisher can ask GAM and Prism in the same turn.

We only bill when a labeled card actually shows. Weather question, no ad. "Any protein powder for lifting this week?" can earn a disclosed card under the reply. Email info@prismpublication.com the campaign you already run. Creative rules are in ad submission.

What does Prism allow, review, or ban?

You keep your own categories. Prism sets what is permitted. A person reads every campaign before it can run. Allowed on this page today: fitness, sleep tools, and productivity. The phone demo is travel packing. Any other niche waits in review. Banned: impersonating the assistant, weapons, explosives, political campaigning, and medical or legal advice sold as a bot recommendation. Gambling stays off wellness bots. Alcohol and adult products stay off bots that are not age-gated 18+.

Fitness

Workout routines, protein goals, bands, and running gear when they are already talking about training. Short questions. A natural place for a product card.

Sleep tools

Sound machines and masks when they want a quieter night, not a diagnosis. Practical sleep aids only.

Productivity

Notes, a daily planner, a task list. The Notion sample is how a software offer should sit next to a reply, without pretending the bot wrote it.

For engineers wiring a bot

How do you attach a bot on Cloudflare Pages?

There is no Prism-hosted secret key. After the bot has an answer, call displayAd from your server. The chat widget only draws HTML you already trust. The matcher reads a static catalog on this Pages site. If matching is too slow, or the question is not close enough, send the reply alone. The developer guide has the server call. Email info@prismpublication.com if you are wiring a live bot.

Call displayAd with the user's question as the topic. You get back a title, description, URL, and a sponsored label that uses the advertiser's brand. That is for the engineer wiring the bot, not for the media brief.

What should a developer quote, and how does a buyer hear it?

Show the ad only when the question is already about that product, decide fast enough that the chat does not freeze, and label every card. The three rows below are the public floors. 0.65 cosine and 120 milliseconds are how we build the matcher. "Close enough" and "the chat does not stall" are how a media lead should hear the same rules. No card means no bill.
